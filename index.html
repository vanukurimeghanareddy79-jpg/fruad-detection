    How FraudShield Analyzes Messages (Beginner Guide)

    1. Pattern & Keyword Scanner

    Uses regular expressions (regex) to search for common scam signals, such as artificial urgency, threats of account blockage, requests for OTPs, and unfamiliar links.

    2. Weighted Risk Scoring

    Assigns risk points based on threat severity. Critical dangers (like OTP requests or remote access apps) contribute more heavily than general urgent words.

    3. Tiered Classification

    Calculates a normalized score (0–100) mapped to 3 clear levels: Low Risk (0–29), Medium Risk (30–59), and High Risk (60–100).
